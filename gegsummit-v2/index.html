03 / GEG Discovery Walk

What is the GEG Summit e-Passport?

The GEG Summit e-Passport is a digital passport concept for the GEG Discovery Walk. Participants explore 11 GEG booths, discover education innovations, try activities and connect with educator communities across Sabah.

Explore the Booths

Visit 11 GEG booths to discover teaching ideas, innovations and community best practices.

Learn and Connect

Take part in demonstrations, learning challenges and networking with educators.

Digital Check-in Concept

The passport is planned to use booth QR codes or PINs to record participation. The official verification method will be announced by the organisers.

This explains the e-Passport concept; it is not a live scanning or official attendance system.

09 / OUR SUMMIT MISSION

Why GEG Summit Sabah?

GEG Summit Sabah aims to inspire and equip education communities across Sabah with practical ideas on how Google applications can accelerate digital transformation in schools and organisations.

The summit also explores artificial intelligence (AI) as a personal assistant that can simplify previously time-consuming and demanding tasks, enabling educators to focus more on learning, creativity and leadership.

Sabah is also home to GEG school communities and educators involved in Google Certified Trainer, Google Certified Coach and Google Certified Innovator programmes. The summit provides a platform to share experiences, build connections and advance innovation together.
